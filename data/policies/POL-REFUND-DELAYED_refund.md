# POL-REFUND-DELAYED

Version: 2026-01
Category: refund

A delayed-delivery refund may be requested only when the order status is
`delayed`. The delay is the number of days between the expected delivery date
and today's date. Gold customers qualify when the delay is strictly greater than
7 days. Standard customers qualify when the delay is strictly greater than
14 days. The refund amount is the recorded order amount.

Support never issues a refund directly. A refund request is always reviewed by a
person; the approval levels are defined in POL-REFUND-APPROVAL.
